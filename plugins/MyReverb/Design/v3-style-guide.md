# MyReverb - Style Guide v3

## Changes from v2
- **Window:** 540 × 440 px (more breathing room)
- **Knobs:** Large 88px / medium 72px (was 72 / 58)
- **Layout:** Header band with flanking metal plates; wider row gaps
- **Branding:** Model plate (TYPE 140A), serial plate (64-LDN), EMI-style stripe

## Knob Sizes (v3)

| Tier | Housing | Knob body | Ticks |
|------|---------|-----------|-------|
| Large | 110×110 | 88px | 13 |
| Medium | 94×94 | 72px | 11 |

## Brand Plates

- **Model plate (left):** Brushed aluminum gradient, embossed sans caps
- **Serial plate (right):** Smaller, serial + "MADE IN ENGLAND"
- Avoid official trademarks; stylistic homage only

## Spacing

- Faceplate padding: 16px
- VU to knobs: 22px
- Between knob rows: 18px
- Inter-knob gap: 32px (large), 28px (medium)
